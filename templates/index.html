Dataset Preprocessing & Exploratory Analysis

Documenting variable selection, imputation rationale, transformations, and feature standardization.

Step 1
Deduplication & Format
Unique EVENT_ID validation; parsed BEGIN and END timestamps with %d-%b-%y %H:%M:%S.
Step 2
Damage USD Parsing
Converted K ($1K), M ($1M), and B ($1B) multipliers into exact floats. Negatives masked to NaN.
Step 3
Missing Value Strategy
Missing damage imputed as $0.00 (NWS protocol: unquantified damage). Missing duration imputed with sample median.
Step 4
Log1p & Scaling
Applied log1p to heavy-tailed features, cyclical sin/cos to month, and scaled with StandardScaler.

Agglomerative Hierarchical Clustering Lab

Comparing Single, Complete, Average, and Ward linkages across Cophenetic correlation and validation metrics.

Selected
Ward's Linkage
Minimizes total within-cluster variance. Produces balanced, compact clusters. Resists chaining.
CPCC: 0.4811 | Sil: 0.3525
Average Linkage
Highest Cophenetic correlation (0.9350). However, isolates extreme outliers into singletons (2496:1).
CPCC: 0.9350 | Chaining Risk
Complete Linkage
Maximizes pairwise distance. Sensitive to noise, isolates individual distant storms.
CPCC: 0.8583 | Outlier splits
Single Linkage
Nearest-neighbor distance. Severe chaining effect; merges dense points into 1 mega-cluster.
